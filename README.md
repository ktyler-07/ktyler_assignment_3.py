The College Life Adventure Game is a simple text game where you play a college student juggling courses, studying, stress, and social life. Your choices affect your GPA, stress, and social points. How it works: 1.) Pick a course load based on your GPA. 2.) Choose a subject to study from a list. The game updates your stats depending on your choices using if/elif/else, membership (in), and logical operators (and, or, not). These are located in both decision codes, but membership is only in the second decision. How to play?: Run the program, enter your information in this order (name, GPA, study hours, social points, stress), pick course load (A/B/C), then choose a subject (Programming, Math, English, or History). There are lots of different endings for this game.
